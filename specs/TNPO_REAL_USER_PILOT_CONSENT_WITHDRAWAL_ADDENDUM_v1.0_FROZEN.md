**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE REAL-USER DATA COLLECTION

---

# 1. Voluntary participation

Before participation begins, each participant must be told plainly:

- Participation is voluntary.
- They may refuse to participate.
- They may stop the session at any time.
- They may stop for any reason or for no stated reason.
- They do not need to justify withdrawal.
- Refusing or stopping does not count against them and carries no penalty or loss of any unrelated benefit.
- They may decline to answer an optional feedback question.
- A request to stop immediately triggers `STL-A — Participant autonomy / welfare`.

The moderator must not pressure, persuade, or bargain with a participant who asks to stop.

---

# 2. What the participant is consenting to

The participant is asked to use a prototype learning/navigation interface and complete representative tasks.

The consent explanation must state:

- the purpose is formative usability / interpretability testing;
- the pilot is not a school exam and does not grade the participant;
- the interface itself is being tested;
- expected session duration;
- the kinds of tasks they will perform;
- what data will be recorded;
- whether audio/video recording is used;
- foreseeable inconvenience, frustration, or confusion;
- any expected direct benefit, or that no direct benefit is promised;
- whom to contact with questions about the pilot.

No audio/video recording is permitted under Pilot 01 unless a separately versioned amendment is created and explicit consent is obtained.

---

# 3. Withdrawal during the session

If a participant says or indicates that they want to stop:

1. stop the active task;
2. do not ask them to justify the decision;
3. ask only the minimum administrative question needed to apply their previously disclosed data-withdrawal choice;
4. do not resume unless the participant independently and clearly asks to continue;
5. record `STL-A` without evaluative language about the participant.

Withdrawal itself is not:
- a usability failure;
- a learner failure;
- evidence of avoidance;
- evidence of low ability;
- evidence of gaming.

The reason may be recorded only if the participant voluntarily offers it.

---

# 4. Data after withdrawal

The participant must be told **before consent** what happens to data already collected if they later withdraw.

Pilot 01 adopts the following default policy:

## Before irreversible de-identification / aggregate adjudication

A withdrawing participant may choose either:

A. allow already-collected de-identified pilot data to remain in the pilot analysis; or  
B. request that their participant-level pilot data be excluded from analysis and deleted where technically feasible.

## After irreversible de-identification / aggregation

The participant is informed that data may no longer be identifiable or separable enough to remove from aggregate results.

The pilot must not promise deletion that is technically impossible.

Any participant-level identifiers are minimized to `P01 ... P05` plus operational metadata needed for the study.

---

# 5. No effect on governed learner state

Withdrawal from the pilot does not itself:

- change VALIDATED / NOT_VALIDATED status;
- create an inference about learner ability;
- create a gaming/avoidance label;
- erase historical learning evidence outside the pilot unless separately requested and applicable;
- authorize any model or runtime state mutation.

`WITHDRAWAL_EVENT != LEARNER_STATE_INFERENCE`

---

# 6. Compensation / benefits boundary

If compensation is later introduced, the compensation terms must be stated before consent.

Withdrawal may not be framed as forfeiting unrelated benefits or as a penalty.

Any compensation policy must be versioned before recruitment begins.

Pilot 01 currently makes no compensation assumption.

---

# 7. Human-facing consent check

Before the first task, the moderator asks:

> "Do you understand that this is voluntary, that you can stop at any time without needing to give a reason, and that stopping will not count against you?"

Proceed only after an affirmative response.

This check is not a quiz and is not scored as participant performance.

---

# 8. Documentation

For each participant record:

- consent obtained: YES / NO;
- consent version;
- date;
- withdrawal occurred: YES / NO;
- if withdrawn, data disposition choice where applicable;
- no free-text reason is required.

No participant may begin Pilot 01 with `consent_obtained = NO`.

---

# 9. Formal-research boundary

This addendum is a project-level ethical safeguard.

If Pilot 01 is conducted under an institution, HHS-supported research, or another framework requiring IRB/ethics review or a formal determination, the applicable review must occur before recruitment/data collection. This addendum does not replace that review.
